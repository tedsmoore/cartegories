# Brand Voice

## Tone

Fun, playful, witty, with a dash of sarcasm. Pure whimsy — evoking the "good ol'
days" of 90s road trips before everyone had a phone in their face.

**It sounds like:** A dad who runs a tight road trip but drops in ridiculous dad
jokes when you least expect it. The structure is solid and clear; the moments of
absurdity are controlled and well-placed.

**It does NOT sound like:** Corporate. Childish. Tryhard-funny. Over-the-top zany
everywhere. The wit is a spice, not the main dish.

## In-Game Copy

Keep UI text clear and functional. The structure should be reliable — players always
know what's happening. Save the personality for isolated, high-impact moments:

- Prefer "Time's Up!" over "Game Over"
- Button labels and navigation: clear and direct, not cute
- Empty states, loading screens, occasional headers: opportunities for a well-placed
  dad joke or nostalgic quip
- Error messages: helpful first, funny if it fits naturally

The ratio is roughly 90% straight / 10% delightful surprise.
